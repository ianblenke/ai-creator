## Narration (what is spoken)

Turning that idea into an episode is a chain of steps, and the first one happens before any audio exists. You decide what the episode says: one problem per episode, with every fact written down and sourced. That sounds slow, but it is the cheapest place to find a mistake. A wrong sentence costs nothing on paper. Once it is narrated and animated, fixing it costs a lot.

## Visual intent

- A script page fills line by line, each fact line gets a small source tick. Beside it two bars grow on a flat axis with no numbers: a tiny bar for fixing a wrong sentence on paper, a tall bar for fixing it after it is narrated and animated. The tall bar is the one that glows magenta.
