## Narration (what is spoken)

The test is simple: what you bring, and what you get. You bring a computer with a decent graphics card, and something you want to explain. I name the smallest card I have tested in the description. You get a real episode, made yourself, in your own voice and only yours, with a few cloud models doing the parts a home card cannot.

## Visual intent

- Two big columns. Left, 'what you bring': a simple computer with a graphics card drawn as a flat slab, and a small spark for the idea. Right, 'what you get': one finished episode frame with a waveform under it, labelled in the narration's words only. A single speech bubble marks 'your own voice and only yours'; a few cloud shapes at the top take small jobs the card cannot.
