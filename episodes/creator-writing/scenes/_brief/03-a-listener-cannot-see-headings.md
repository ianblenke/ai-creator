## Narration (what is spoken)

The first stop is the heading itself. On a page, a heading tells the reader the subject has changed, and out loud there is no heading, so nothing does. So if one scene ends on the kettle's switch and the next opens on the handle, with nothing joining them, it sounds as though the narrator stopped talking and started on something else. I know that feeling from the other side: a viewer of my earlier video series told me one of my videos just stops talking and changes the subject. My rule had been that every scene must tie back to the last one, but a bare question counted, and a bare question bridges nothing.

## Visual intent

- A page with three scene headings down the left that fade away, leaving the text running on with a visible gap between two topics. A listener figure faces the gap with a small magenta question mark over it. Then the headings return, dim, with a cross over them.
