## Narration (what is spoken)

But before you record, remember that a script that passes both reviews has still only passed a reading, because both reviewers read text and neither can hear. I do not think they can tell whether a sentence sounds like you, whether a joke lands, or what you actually believe. So you read the script out loud yourself once, and you keep your own opinions in it, said plainly as yours: "I think", not "it is". Reading it aloud does one more job: you hear the phrases that are hard to say. That matters if, like me, you record with a cloned copy of your own voice, which stumbles in its own ways. Its checks come in a later part.
