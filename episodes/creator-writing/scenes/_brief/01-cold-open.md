# Scene brief: 01-cold-open

Write `scenes/01-cold-open.js` following docs/scene-contract.md, the series bible (scenes/_brief/_bible.md) and tools/stage/style.js.

## Narration (what is spoken)

Here are two ways to say one thing about a made-up kettle. The first: this kettle has a thermostat, a steel base and a lid that locks. The second: this kettle switches itself off when the water boils, so you can walk away from it. The first is every fact I had. The second is the reason you would care. A script written like the first reads fine on a page, but it sounds like a list when someone hears it. This part is about catching that before you record a word.

## Visual intent

- Two cards side by side. The left card shows three small tags in a row with no connection between them: a thermostat, a steel base, a locked lid. The right card shows one kettle with a single line from the boiling water to a switch, and a small tick. A listener figure with its ears lit stands between them, leaning toward the right card.
