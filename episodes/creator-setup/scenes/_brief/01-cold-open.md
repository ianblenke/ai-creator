# Scene brief: 01-cold-open

Write `scenes/01-cold-open.js` following docs/scene-contract.md, the series bible (scenes/_brief/_bible.md) and tools/stage/style.js.

Duration: 24.62 s (scene-local time 0..duration; hold the final state for ~0.6 s after).

## Narration (what is spoken)

The example in this part needs no graphics card, no voice server and, once the tools are built, no internet connection. Its narration is silent, on purpose: a placeholder with the right length, so the pictures and the video come out exactly as they will with a real voice. You can see that work before you record or clone your own voice.

## Visual intent

- A plain computer with no card beside it. A flat, silent line where a waveform would be, and a video frame builds up on the screen anyway, step by step. Calm, sparse, near-black.
