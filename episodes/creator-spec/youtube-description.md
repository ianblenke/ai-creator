## Description

Before this series records a single word, it decides what one video says, where every fact comes from, and what must never appear. This part covers the one-page plan, the fact sheet with its cited, measured and estimated markers, the private files that stay outside the project, the script format, the safety check that stops a script before it reaches the voice, and the two git hooks that guard your history. It ends on what none of that can catch, which you still check yourself.

In the opening, I planted a made-up machine name, a made-up address and a made-up port in a script on purpose. The safety check blocked the recording and told me which line and what kind of thing it found. That was one run of my own, on 2026-11-03.

The check only matches text patterns: it does not know your street or your employer unless they are on your list, and it cannot see a face or a reflection in a window.
