## Narration (what is spoken)

None of this is complete, because the check only knows what you have taught it, and it only matches patterns. It does not know your street or your employer unless they are on the list. It cannot catch a place described in words, a face, a reflection in a window, or the terminal line that shows your user and machine name. So you look at every frame yourself, and you run the picture text scan by hand on every recording; it needs an extra tool, and without it, it checks nothing.

## Visual intent

- The shield from before with four small gaps in its outline: a place described in words, a face, a window reflection, a terminal line. A pair of eyes moves across a row of frames. The last frame shows a listener figure beside a script with its headings crossed out.
