## Narration (what is spoken)

The check guards what you make, but not what you save into your project's history, so two small git hooks guard that step. You turn them on once, with one command. The first refuses a short list of files by name: your settings, your list of names to block, your voice files, and anything with an audio or video extension. It never reads what is inside a file, so the check above is still your text check. The second deletes one kind of line from a commit message, the one an AI coding assistant adds to link back to its chat. Every other line stays, so read the message yourself, and know that skipping the hooks skips both.

## Visual intent

- A commit box with two small gates in front of it. The first gate stops a folder labelled private and a film strip, and lets a script page through. The second gate removes one bar from a message and leaves the others. A switch marked 'on once' is lit beside them.
