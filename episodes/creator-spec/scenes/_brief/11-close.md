## Narration (what is spoken)

That is the whole plan for an episode: a one-page plan, a fact sheet, a script, and a check that reads all of it before anything is recorded. That is the end of this part. In the next part, we start on the script itself: writing for the ear, with a reviewer who reads it as a listener who cannot see the headings.

## Visual intent

- The plan page, the fact sheet and the shield as three small icons in a calm row. They hold for a moment, then dim. A title card fades in: "Next: writing for the ear". An unlabeled arrow points right, and the frame holds still through the tail.
