## Description

Every stage of making a narrated video hands one job to one model. This part names each one, says what it is good at and what it cannot do, and says which run on your own machine and which you pay for. It also covers two audio-listening models that failed my test, and why a recognizer never hears a sniff.

What the video covers: the voice, the two checkers that listen to it, the models that did not work as listeners, the cloud model that writes the pictures, and the program that runs the rest. These are the models in early November of 2026, and they will change.

Code, docs and the example: https://github.com/ianblenke/ai-creator (this video matches the tag part-01)

How this was made
The narration is a cloned copy of my own voice, generated with Audio8-TTS-Realistic 0.6B from my own reference clip. It is not a recording of me reading; the model adds no watermark, so I am saying so here.
Voice model: Audio8-TTS-Realistic 0.6B by Edge0; Apache-2.0 license (read 2026-11-01), the model card does not describe its training data: https://huggingface.co/Edge0/Audio8-TTS-Realistic-0.6b
License pages for the aligner and the recognizer were read on 2026-10-28; the pricing page on 2026-11-02. Check each page yourself, and plans change.
Pictures were written by a cloud model and checked by me; I decide what each episode says. Posted as made; ask for a part in the comments.
