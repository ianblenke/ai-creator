ADVERSARIAL VISUAL REVIEW: "How a Kettle Knows When to Switch Off" (9 scenes, contact sheets)
Frame times below are estimated from the visible labels and the brief's word times; the sheets are not timestamped.

1. Scene 05 (the snap), HIGH: the chain reaction is not animated in sync with the narration, and the scene goes dead for its second half. The rod, lever and contacts first appear at "one common design" (about 12 s). By the next frame (about 13 s) the small disc has flipped, the lever is already tipped, the contacts are already apart and the power circle is already OFF. From "the snap pushes a rod" (13.3 s) through "tips over once it goes past the middle" (18.7 s), "pulls the electrical contacts apart" (22.3 s) and "the power is cut cleanly" (26.4 s), the mechanism does not move: the lever angle, the contact gap and the power state are identical in all 10 remaining frames. Only labels change. So "past the middle" shows a dashed balance line under a lever that is already past it, and "open sharply instead of slowly" shows nothing opening. The disc also snaps TWICE in this scene: once as the big close-up (about 9 to 11 s), then again as the small disc under the rod. FIX: show the mechanism with the disc still cold (contacts closed, power ON, lever on the near side of level) and drive everything from one flip timed to "the snap pushes a rod" (13.3 s). Then hold the lever just short of the balance line until "goes past the middle" (18.7 s) and tip it there. Have the contacts spring open on "apart" (22.3 s) and the power circle go dark on "cut" (26.1 s). If snapSwitch only couples to the curl, run the close-up flip on the snapSwitch disc itself (one disc, one flip), or put the rod/lever animation on the camera and spotlight timing so each part lights up as it is named. Make the open contact gap at least 3 times its current size: it is about 30 px now and hard to see.

2. Scene 01 (the hook), MEDIUM: the "button clicking down, then popping back up by itself" is the hook's central picture, but the rocker on the base is about 40 px. It can only be read through the power dot and a few tiny spark ticks on "it stops". It also clicks down at about 2 s ("something clever"), before "heats water" (4.1 s). FIX: glide the camera in on the base for the click-down at "heats water" and for the pop-up at "it stops" (7.9 s), scaling the switch to at least 150 px on screen, then pull back.

3. Scene 02 (the steam), MEDIUM, continuity: the sensor housing on top of the handle (with its amber/blue disc) is visible throughout scene 01, but it is missing from the first 8 frames of scene 02 (plain handle). It reappears at "a small pipe" as an empty white box, and the disc pops in at "sensor". The kettle must look the same everywhere. FIX: keep the housing and disc on screen from frame 1 but dimmed, and spotlight them (brighten plus a soft glow) on "pipe" and "sensor" instead of making them appear.

4. Scene 02 (the steam), MEDIUM, the key mechanism is not legible: steam is never seen travelling along the pipe into the sensor. The channel is a short stub near the top of the wall, and the inside wisps never visibly enter it. That flow is the whole point of the scene ("a small pipe carries that steam to a sensor"). FIX: glide the camera in on the channel at "carries that steam" (12.0 s) and run k.steamToSensor with a higher amount and larger wisps, so 2 or 3 wisps visibly travel from the steam space through the wall into the housing. Then light up the sensor.

5. Scene 02 (the steam), MEDIUM, physical ambiguity: at "not in the water" a dashed cool-blue line appears just under the sensor housing, between the wall and the handle, far above the actual water line. A blue dashed line reads as "the water comes up to here", which contradicts the narration. FIX: remove it, or put the separation marker AT the real water surface and draw a clear vertical gap (bracket or double arrow) between the water line and the channel.

6. Scene 06 (the second safety), MEDIUM: the guard's trip and the weld are not readable. In the inset, the "snap" of the violet disc is a small arc change. "Cuts the power" is shown only as one vertical stroke splitting into two thin strokes. "Contacts weld shut" is a tiny magenta dot on one stroke. The coil also re-heats and the power comes back ON at "weld shut", then goes OFF again after the narration ends, a sequence the narration never explains. FIX: make the inset larger, with the guard at least 300 px wide on screen. Use contacts(...).set({open}) at a readable size for the trip and contacts(...).set({weld}) with a clear fused blob for the weld. For the weld, either do not switch the power back on (just show that the guard's contacts can no longer open and the backup flips), or add the backup's trip within the narration window ("acts only if the guard itself fails", about 28.4 to 29.7 s).

7. Scene 07 (the reset), MEDIUM, physics and sync: the disc springs back at about 6.5 to 7 s, right as cooling starts. The thermometer dot has barely left the "switching temperature" mark, and the gap bracket only becomes full later, during "cooled about thirty degrees below" (9 to 11.5 s). The picture says it resets immediately; the narration says it resets only after a large drop. FIX: show a flipped disc that cools (glow fades) while the dot slides down the full gap from 5 s to about 9.5 s, and flip it back (one 0.15 s flip) only when the dot reaches the bottom of the bracket, around "below" (10.6 s). Move the "springs back by itself" label to that moment.

8. Scene 07 (the reset), MEDIUM: "you press a reset knob" has no press and no hand. The "knob" is a white rounded rectangle on a stalk sticking out of the base under the spout, which reads as a plug or a toggle. This protrusion also exists only in this scene, a kettle continuity break. FIX: draw a small round push button on the base's front (scene-level, not a new kettle silhouette). On "press" (13.6 s), show a simple hand or fingertip shape pressing it, with the button travelling in, and the sensor disc flipping back to cold or the power becoming available.

9. Scene 07 (the reset), MEDIUM, legibility: the final wide shot shrinks the side labels ("springs back by itself", "switching temperature", "reset knob", "one design", "other designs") to roughly 36 px or less, under the 38 px floor. The same happens to the inset labels in scene 06 ("second small disc", "backup", also crowded on one baseline) and to the "boils cooler" pill in scene 03. FIX: in the final wide frames, fade the small side labels out (keep only the big restating phrase), or counter-scale labels against the camera zoom. Run layout-check on the camera-scaled output.

Scene 05 (the snap), LOW-MEDIUM: at the cut from the close-up to the mechanism (about 12 s), the big disc fades out as a faint rainbow arc right over the lever while the small disc is in a different (flat) shape: two discs in two states on screen at once, overlapping the mechanism. FIX: zoom the camera out from the big disc to the small disc so it is visibly the same disc (same curl), or fade the big disc fully before the mechanism appears.

The last frame also restates only "helps on a mountain", not the scene's main point (it trips well under the boil). FIX: hold the camera still for the mountain beat. (b) The disc's snap lands about 1 to 1.5 s after the spoken "snaps" (1.4 s): it is still flat at about 2 s. (c) During the crossfade, the toaster slides in touching the kettle's handle and sensor. FIX: drop "the next part". Time the flip to "snaps" (1.4 s). Slide the toaster in only after the kettle has faded, or keep at least 80 px of clearance.

13. Scenes 01 to 02, 05 to 06, 06 to 07, LOW, continuity of state: each scene silently resets the kettle's state. Scene 01 ends switched OFF with calm water, and scene 02 opens boiling with the power ON. Scene 05 ends with the power OFF, and scene 06 opens full, boiling and ON. Scene 06 ends empty with a hot coil, and scene 07 opens full again. A reset between scenes is acceptable, but a hard cut reads like an edit error. FIX: open each of these scenes with a 0.5 to 1 s visible transition (water refilling, or the click going down and the coil warming), or start from the previous end state and change it on the first relevant word.

14. Scene 01 (the hook), LOW: steam keeps rising from the spout for about 12 s after the kettle has switched off and the water is calm. The bible says steam appears only while boiling or just after. "Something clever" is a weak label, the narration's words but not a concept. FIX: fade the steam to faint wisps after "it stops" and bring it up again only on "rising from the spout" (18.4 s). Drop the "something clever" label, or replace it with the click itself.

15. Scene 06 (the second safety), LOW: the final big phrase "a separate part guards against that" relies on "that", which refers to something 20 s earlier, after the narration has moved on to the backup and the weld. FIX: end on a narration phrase that stands alone, such as "a further backup" or "a second small disc that snaps", or keep "guards against that" but show the empty kettle prominently in that frame so "that" has a visible referent.

16. Scene 08 (the close), LOW: the four recap icons are small (about 200 px) in a mostly empty frame, and "The story is simple" stays as a title for about 10 s without adding anything. The "bimetal disc" icon is a curved dish at first and later a flat strip, an inconsistent shape. FIX: make the icons about 1.5 times larger and fade the title after "simple" (about 2.5 s). Keep the disc icon in the cold dish shape throughout (the snap icon carries the flipped shape).

17. Scene 02 (the steam), LOW: "the boil has begun" is set in magenta, the colour the bible reserves for heat and danger, and the label style (plain text) differs from scene 03's pill labels. FIX: use ink or accent for non-heat labels, and pick one label style (plain or pill) for the whole video.

What works: the kettle silhouette, orientation (spout left, handle right), sensor position on the handle, water colour, steam style and the two-layer disc (amber on top and inside when cold, amber on the outside when flipped) are consistent across scenes. In scene 04, "one expands faster" is shown with longer amber arrows, and the disc flattens slowly as described. The guard is violet, pressed against the underside of the coil, with a fainter violet backup to its right. No digits appear on screen; scene 07 shows the drop as a gap marker. Most labels appear within about 0.5 s of their word, and every scene ends on a big restating phrase.

Score: 3/5. The look is consistent and clean, but the core payoff (scene 05's snap chain) is static and already finished before it is narrated, and several mechanisms (the steam pipe, the guard trip, the weld, the reset) are too small or not timed to read.
