## Narration (what is spoken)

Every morning a kettle does something clever, and you never notice. It heats water until the water boils, and then, with nobody touching it, it stops. It has no clock to count minutes and no screen to read, so how does a plain kettle know the water is ready? In many kettles, the answer begins with something you can see rising from the spout.

## Visual intent

- A kettle on a counter, a button clicking down, then the click popping back up by itself. A faint question mark over the lid. Wisps of steam drift from the spout.
