## Narration (what is spoken)

That cloud from the spout is the clue the kettle uses too. Before the boil, only a little steam forms, so a burst of it is a fast and clear sign that the boil has begun. In many kettles, a small pipe carries that steam to a sensor. The sensor sits in the steam and not in the water, so it reacts to the boil itself.

## Visual intent

- Cutaway of a kettle. Bubbles rise, steam gathers at the top and flows along a thin pipe to a small sensor, drawn as a glowing dot. The water level stays clearly separate from the sensor.
