# Series bible: "How a Kettle Knows When to Switch Off" (the AI Creator example episode)

Look: AI Creator "Signal": near-black background, acid green and magenta accents, big type, flat shapes, generous space. Use `palette` from "style" (already set to Signal) and the kit from `docs/visual-kit.md` / `tools/stage/kit.js`. Calm, one idea per scene.

## Shared vocabulary (the same colour means the same thing in every scene)
- THE KETTLE = an ink (white, `ink`) outline, a simple jug with a spout and a handle; the same silhouette in every scene it appears. WATER = `cool` blue. STEAM = soft `ink` wisps at low opacity (never a solid shape). THE HEATING ELEMENT = a coil near the bottom: dim when cold, `hot` (magenta) when heating. HEAT/ DANGER = `hot`.
- THE SENSOR DISC = a shallow dish made of TWO LAYERS: top layer `warm` (amber), bottom layer `cool` (blue). When it bends, the amber layer is the one that stretches more. When it flips it does so in one fast move (about 0.15 s), not a slow tween.
- POWER / THE SWITCH = a small circle. ON = `accent` (acid green), OFF = dim outline. The kettle's power state is always shown this way.
- THE SECOND SAFETY (guard) disc = the same dish shape as the sensor disc but outlined in `violet`, so the viewer can tell the two discs apart; the backup is a fainter `violet` outline.
- MOUNTAIN = a simple ink outline silhouette; the thermometer mark is a thin `warm` line.
- Labels: the narration's own words only, at most 5 words, at least 38 px (kit `type`). Never any brand, number or place that the narration does not say. NO numbers on screen at all, except if the narration says "thirty degrees" in the reset scene (write "about 30 degrees below" is NOT allowed on screen: show the idea as a gap marker with no digits).

## Hard rules
- Show only what the scene's narration and visual notes say. Every cue anchor must be a phrase that appears VERBATIM in the scene's narration (verify with `node tools/check-anchors.js examples/ep01-kettle`).
- Scenes are pure functions of t: no Date, Math.random (use `ctx.rand(seed)`), fetch, timers, CSS/SMIL animation. No text over text (fade one fully out before the next in). Keep content above y = 900 (subtitle strip). No serif font.
- Calm background: use `paintBackground` + `makeParticles` from the kit.
- The last frame restates the scene's point in one big phrase (at least 56 px) and is held through the 0.6 s tail; end wide.
- Intro-like and outro scenes are calm and short; the outro has a series title tag "AI Creator" in `accent` and a small unlabeled silhouette of the next object, nothing else.
- NOTHING may be cut by the frame edge at ANY time (even dimmed): fade items out or reframe.

## SHARED KETTLE (authoritative; overrides any brief or earlier wording)
Every scene draws the kettle and its parts ONLY with `examples/ep01-kettle/scenes/_kettle.js`: `import { kettle, sensorDisc, guardDisc, powerLight, steam, contacts, snapSwitch, KETTLE, SNAP, POWER_SPOT, CURL, FLIP_DUR, bendCurl, flipCurl } from './_kettle.js';`. Do not draw your own kettle, disc, steam, power circle or switch mechanism; change only position, scale (`.at(x, y, s)`), `show`, `dim` and the state methods. Put these Items in `stage.world` and move the camera to them.
- KETTLE: `kettle(parent)` is the one cutaway jug: spout LEFT, handle RIGHT, NEVER mirrored (no negative scale, no rotation), lid with knob, base, coil near the bottom. State: `water(level)` (0 empty .. 1 highest; the water line always stays well below the steam channel), `boil(t, a)` (bubbles; call after `water`), `heat(h)` (coil dim -> `hot`), `click(p)` (the rocker switch on the base: 1 down = switched on, 0 popped up), `power(p)`. `k.toWorld(KETTLE.sensor)` (also `KETTLE.guard`, `KETTLE.element`, `KETTLE.power`, `KETTLE.spoutTip`) gives world points for camera moves and hand-offs.
- SENSOR: one fixed place: a small housing on TOP of the handle (`KETTLE.sensor`), fed by a short steam channel (`k.channel`) that starts in the steam space ABOVE the water and passes through the wall. Steam reaches it with `k.steamToSensor.update(t, a)`. The kettle's sensor is `k.sensor`, the same two-layer disc as the close-up `sensorDisc`. A close-up "zoom into the sensor" scales a `sensorDisc` up from `k.toWorld(KETTLE.sensor)`, never from anywhere else.
- DISC CURL (one convention, every scene): `.curl(k)`: `CURL.cold` = +1 = dish with the CENTRE LOWEST (amber on top, on the inside); heating bends it gradually toward `CURL.trip` (`bendCurl(t, start, dur)`, slow); the snap is `flipCurl(t, at)`: one 0.15 s flip to `CURL.flipped` = -1, centre HIGHEST, amber on the outside. The reset is `flipCurl(t, at, CURL.flipped, CURL.cold)`. Never tween a flip slowly; never start a scene with the disc in a shape the previous scene did not end on.
- POWER: one circle. On the kettle it is fixed just right of the base (`k.power(p)`); in a scene without the kettle use `powerLight(world).at(POWER_SPOT.x, POWER_SPOT.y)` (and grow it with scale if needed). 1 = ON `accent` with a soft ring, 0 = OFF dim outline. It is never spotlight-dimmed (`dim` is a no-op on it and `k.dim` leaves it bright).
- STEAM: only `steam(parent, { from, to, n, period, sway, size })` and the kettle's `k.steamOut` (spout), `k.steamIn` (space above the water), `k.steamToSensor` (channel); `.update(t, amount)`. Low-opacity ink wisps, never solid. Steam appears only while the water is boiling or just after.
- GUARD and BACKUP: `k.guard` (violet, the same dish shape) pressed against the underside of the heating coil; `k.backup` (fainter violet) beside it, to its right. Both hidden until shown (`.show(a)`); they flip with `flipCurl` like the sensor. For a big view zoom the camera to `k.toWorld(KETTLE.guard)` (the guard must be at least 200 px wide on screen when it trips) or draw a large `guardDisc(world, { w, faint })` next to a large coil. Contacts that open or weld shut: `contacts(parent).set({ open, weld })`.
- THE SNAP MECHANISM: `snapSwitch(parent).set({ curl, middle })`: the disc drives everything. The rod stands on the disc centre, the seesaw lever's left end rests on the rod, and once the lever passes level (`middle` shows the dashed balance line) its right end pushes the moving contact down and the contacts open (`it.open` 0..1 after `set`: drive the power circle from it). Cold dish = contacts closed; flipped = open. Never move the rod, lever or contacts independently of the disc.
- No text is drawn by the module; labels stay scene-level (38 px or more, narration words, no numbers).
